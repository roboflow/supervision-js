Oriented rectangle editing

Draw a rectangle, then select it to resize in its local axes or drag the small rotation control. Hold Shift to snap rotation to 15°. Escape cancels an edit. Scroll to zoom.

Enter an angle to preview; Enter or leaving the field saves it. Focus Rotate with arrow keys, then use ← / → for 1° (Shift: 15°). Escape cancels a preview. With heading enabled, double-click an edge or its midpoint handle to make it the front. The small triangle marks the front; choosing a front keeps the footprint.
